# The Story So Far

The background of *The Ur-Quan Masters*, as the player learns it: from the
opening slideshow, the game manual, and what Starbase Commander Hayes tells
Captain Zelnick when asked about history. Nothing here goes beyond what the
player can hear early in the game.

## Who you are

*From the manual ([`doc/release/UQM-Manual.txt`](../release/UQM-Manual.txt), "The Story So Far").*

For a decade, Earth and the rest of the Alliance of Free Stars fought the
Ur-Quan and their Hierarchy of Battle Thralls. During the War, humans found a
factory world built by the **Precursors**, an impossibly advanced race that
disappeared tens of thousands of years ago. The colony there, **Unzervalt**
(Vela I), lost all contact with Earth shortly after landfall.

You are **Captain Zelnick**, a human born on Unzervalt with a remarkable
knack for Precursor technology. You worked out how to activate the
installation. Unzervalt is mineral-poor, so the factory could only build part
of a ship. Your task is to command that craft, the **Vindicator**, back to
Earth to tell them of the lost colony, and, if the War continues, to fight for
Earth and the Alliance. The game begins in **2155**.

## What Commander Hayes tells you

*From the Earth Starbase dialogue ([`content/base/comm/starbase/starbase.txt`](../../content/base/comm/starbase/starbase.txt)).*

When the Vindicator reaches Sol, Earth is not free. Commander Hayes runs the
starbase in orbit, and fills in what happened while the colony was cut off.

### How the War began

- **2098.** The Chenjesu's super-sensitive receivers pick up a strange signal
  from the Ophiuchi constellation: the Ur-Quan's "exulting hunting cry" on
  detecting many sentient species. It is the first sign of their arrival.
- The Ur-Quan conquer the **Umgah**, a solitary but not unfriendly species,
  then turn on the hostile but weak **Ilwrath**. A hastily assembled force of
  **Mmrnmhrm** and **Chenjesu** ships turns them aside, so they subjugate the
  **Spathi** instead. With each conquest the Ur-Quan fleet grows, adding slave
  vessels to its ranks.
- **2112.** The Chenjesu arrive in Earth's solar system for the first time.
  Earth joins them to form the **Alliance of Free Stars** at about the time
  the **Androsynth** fall to the Ur-Quan.
- **2116.** Before the ink is dry on the treaty, a new race appears in orbit
  around the Moon and asks to join the Alliance: the **Ariloulaleelay**.

### How the War went

- Earth turns out hundreds of heavy Cruisers. Meanwhile the Ur-Quan move on
  the **VUX**, known only to the Yehat.
- **2119.** Humanity's botched first contact with the VUX: Captain Jeffry L.
  Rand of the *Far Voyager* offends the VUX commander with an inadvertent
  insult. Hayes calls it the biggest single mistake of the War.
- After defeating the VUX, the Ur-Quan run into the combined might of the
  **Yehat** and **Shofixti**, backed by the first human Cruisers. They turn
  away again, and find the **Mycon**, who submit voluntarily and bring a
  hundred Mycon Podships to the Hierarchy.
- The last to join are the **Syreen**, space gypsies who escaped the
  Hierarchy by moving their fleet of slow habitats into human space.

### How the War was lost

- The final Ur-Quan offensive comes through VUX space at the Indi and Mira
  systems. It is barely repulsed, and for ten years the Coreward Front holds.
- **2134.** The balance shifts, at about the time the research mission was
  sent to Vela. The Alliance is pushed back beyond Raynet. Holding Rigel costs
  the Chenjesu grievously, and the Ur-Quan focus on Procyon. That is the last
  anyone hears of the Chenjesu and the Mmrnmhrm.
- Weeks later, Hierarchy ships attack from every direction. Ceres Station
  falls, then the laser forts on the Moon. Dreadnoughts take position above
  Rome, Moscow, Beijing, Tokyo, London, Buenos Aires and Washington, and the
  Ur-Quan make their point by erasing Buenos Aires from the map.

### After the surrender

- The UN surrenders, and humanity is given a week to choose the nature of its
  servitude, by popular vote. Earth chooses **not** to fight for the Ur-Quan,
  and becomes a **fallow slave world**.
- Everyone has a month to withdraw to Earth; anything left off-planet will be
  destroyed when the slave shield goes up.
- The Ur-Quan then destroy every human structure more than 500 years old:
  much of London and Paris, the Giza Pyramids, the Parthenon, Stonehenge, and
  targets nobody suspected were significant, in Iraq, the Amazon, under the
  Antarctic ice and on the Atlantic floor. In 40 hours, most of humanity's
  history is gone.
- Then the **slave shield** goes up and contact with the outside world ends.
  Hayes himself didn't see the stars again until he was posted to the
  starbase, 8 years before you arrive.

### The races, as Hayes knew them

**The Alliance of Free Stars**

| Race | Hayes's view |
|---|---|
| **Chenjesu** | Silicon-based, deeply intelligent, the Alliance's leaders in all but name. Communicate over HyperWave naturally. |
| **Mmrnmhrm** | Robots built by a giant Factory-Ark ("Mother-Ark") sent by an unknown culture centuries ago, maybe as the vanguard of a colonization project. |
| **Yehat** | Ancient warrior clans, loyal to the Queen and her royal family, the Veep-Zeeps, under whom they have never lost a battle in 2,000 years. |
| **Shofixti** | Intelligent marsupials uplifted by the Yehat. Noble, fearless warriors who breed fast, and carry the "Glory Device". |
| **Ariloulaleelay** | Unsettling, secretive allies with an unexplained way of crossing HyperSpace very fast. |
| **Syreen** | A peaceful culture from the Bronze Age to starflight, until their Eden-like home world was destroyed in a cataclysm. |

**The Hierarchy of Battle Thralls**

| Race | Hayes's view |
|---|---|
| **Ur-Quan** | Cunning, relentless, technologically superior. They live only to subjugate other intelligent life, using no more force than they need, and never stop moving. |
| **Mycon** | Thrive only near the melting point of lead, and their corpses can revive after thawing. Nobody has had a real conversation with one. The only race known to *seek* enslavement. |
| **Spathi** | "A cowardly, mobile clam armed with a howitzer", though tough once cornered. Rumours of a brave "Black Spathi Squadron". |
| **Umgah** | Big, chatty blobs that fell early in the War. The Arilou hinted at a prior relationship with them. |
| **Androsynth** | The most feared enemy on the Coreward Front. Humanity's own creations, kept as slaves for nearly 50 years before they rebelled. |
| **VUX** | Enemies thanks to Captain Rand's insult at first contact. |
| **Ilwrath** | Spider-like zealots who sacrifice prisoners to Dogar and Kazon, their twin gods of destruction and torment. |

He also knows of two other starfaring races the Chenjesu had met, and an
alien race the Arilou once "had some fun" with, but nothing more.

### Older history

- **The Precursors.** About 200,000 years ago, a starfaring species appeared
  and spread everywhere, leaving relics from an orbital platform at Alpha
  Centauri to DataPlates in a cave on Pluto. Judging by their equipment, they
  were giants, 5 to 8 metres tall. About 3,000 years after appearing, they
  vanished in less than a decade.
- **An older empire.** A Chenjesu historian, Tzzz-Tzer-Tzak, found evidence
  of an interstellar empire of several races about 22,000 years ago. The only
  member that lived in this region was a rock-like species in the Vulpeculae
  constellation, whose name he never learned.
- **Aliens on Earth.** Many 20th-century UFO "encounters" describe beings
  that match the Ariloulaleelay almost perfectly, including examinations and
  repeat "check-up" visits. Nobody ever got to ask the Arilou why.
